About

Researcher,
maker, listener.

I’m Riddhi Taunk, a UX Design Researcher based in London. My practice sits between fieldwork and making — studying how people move, feel and relate to the spaces and objects around them.

Currently at the University of the Arts London, my work draws on community listening and participatory methods. I spend time with the people a design is meant to serve before a single decision is made, translating lived experience into research that holds up under scrutiny.

The through-line is the body: tactile design, ergonomics and sensory environments — the quiet, physical details that shape behaviour long before anyone notices them. I work with institutions, studios and communities who care about getting those details right.

If that sounds like your kind of work, the door is open.
