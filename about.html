About

I make work for people who want to slow down and pay attention. Sound, story, and the design that holds them together.

My practice sits between the studio and the page — audio essays, documentary, editorial design, and the occasional installation. The common thread is restraint: black ink, warm paper, generous space, and a belief that the most interesting thing in the room is usually the one you have to lean in to hear.

I work with publications, artists, and institutions who care about craft. If that sounds like you, the door is open.
